1. Direct Current (DC) Circuits & Internal Resistance

Real voltage sources possess internal resistance (r). This dictates that terminal voltage available drops as current increases due to internal "lost volts".

ε = V_terminal + Ir
V_terminal = I R_ext
Lost Volts = I r
Official Moderation Feedback Warning: Candidates lose marks by confusing EMF (ε) with Terminal Voltage. EMF is constant for a cell, but terminal voltage drops dynamically as current scales up.

2. Capacitors & Dielectrics

Inserting a dielectric material enhances capacitance by polarizing its molecules, creating an internal opposing electric field that lowers total net potential difference for a fixed charge.

C = ε_0 ε_r A / d
Q = CV
E = 1/2 Q V = 1/2 C V²
Unexpected Exam Blueprint Trap: Markers note that when a dielectric is manipulated, students fail to state whether the capacitor remains connected to a battery (V is constant) or is isolated (Q is constant).
